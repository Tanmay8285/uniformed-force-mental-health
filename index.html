HOW THE PERSONAL SIGNAL IS CALCULATED

A transparent weighted score (0–100) combines self-rated stress, fatigue, sleep, workload, mood and support. It is a demo signal, not a validated prediction, diagnosis or operational risk assessment. Real ML would need representative consented data, clinical validation and ongoing bias testing.
